DP5q numerical assets

These float32 parameters were exported without training from the DP5 models
at revision 8f8e22364654bbce71dcb6abb414eee793b00d92. Duplicate encoder tensors
were stored once; two distinct prediction heads are retained. The NumPy
implementation, safe numeric container and JSON metadata are modifications.
The original Goodman Lab MIT license is included in LICENSE.dp5.txt.
No training dataset, optimizer state, executable model, pickle or NFP code is
included in these numeric assets.
